1. Possessive Adjectives

Indican a quién le pertenece algo.

I ➡️ my (mi)
You ➡️ your (tu)
He ➡️ his (su, de él)
She ➡️ her (su, de ella)
It ➡️ its (su, de animal/cosa)
We ➡️ our (nuestro)
They ➡️ their (su, de ellos)

2. Demonstrative Pronouns

Cerca ⬇️

  • This: Este/Esta (1 cosa)
  • These: Estos/Estas (Varias)

Lejos ➡️

  • That: Ese/Esa (1 cosa)
  • Those: Esos/Esas (Varias)
